A little more about me and how I work

I'm a UI/UX designer and Information Systems graduate with hands-on experience designing digital products. During my internships at Ukirama and Paragon, I worked on company websites, ERP modules, and admin, agency, and creator portals. My work spans designing, prototyping, and usability testing. Outside of internships, I've won Best Mobile App Design at VWO 2024 and placed in other national UI/UX competitions.

I also work on design systems. At Paragon, I helped build a Figma design system with 150+ color tokens and 100+ variables, and worked with the engineering team on documentation that makes the design system easier to use in development. At Ukirama, I audited the website's UI components and colors, then worked with the team to decide which elements to keep and standardize.

Before and alongside design, I did QA testing, learned front-end development, and worked with data. That helps me design things developers can build and spot problems early. I also led the academic division of a student Android study club and helped organize faculty events, so I'm used to working in a team and meeting deadlines.
